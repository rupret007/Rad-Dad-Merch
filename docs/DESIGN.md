# Design language

## Collection identity

**Rad Dad // Retro Riot NFC Collection**

**Old media. Loud band. One tap.**

The collection should feel like a punk merch table discovered inside an old
video store. Every object must remain recognizable before the viewer notices
the Rad Dad branding.

## Design rules

1. Authentic silhouette first. Branding never replaces the defining hardware.
2. Bold details at keychain scale. Avoid decoration thinner than a 0.4 mm
   nozzle can reproduce reliably.
3. One-color depth. Recesses, raised borders, and stepped surfaces create the
   visual hierarchy normally supplied by multiple materials.
4. Punk, not careless. Uneven energy belongs in the character and typography,
   while dimensions, clearances, and NFC surfaces remain deliberate.
5. Hardware belongs in the composition. Eyelets and bases should look designed
   rather than attached afterward.
6. Every piece earns its space. Remove details that do not improve identity,
   durability, readability, or function.

## Tap affordance

The media fronts stay visually authentic. The primary tap instruction lives on
the 25 mm rear NFC label, where `TAP TO PLAY` is impossible to mistake for a
decorative media marking. A matching handoff card explains the interaction at
the moment the collectible is received. Trailer Swift also carries a small
`TAP THE BASE` stage cue because its NFC sticker is underneath the stand.

## v6 decisions

### Mini VHS v3

The earlier version shared too much of the cassette's visual hierarchy. v3
uses a wide shell, a dominant hinged tape-door band, a framed reel bay,
nine-tooth hubs, a center tape gauge, five screw bosses, and a compact label.
The result reads as VHS before the lettering is noticed.

### Floppy v18

The front remains familiar and readable. The rear now includes the shell seam,
hub boundary, shutter track, write-protect hardware, and orientation marks.
All rear engraving stays outside the full 25 mm NFC adhesion circle.

### Trailer Swift v4

Trailer Swift is a one-piece upright collectible rather than a flat keychain.
The comic figure is thickened with rounded toy-like rear volumes and a faux
bobble spring. It stands in a solid stepped circular base with a front
`TRAILER SWIFT` nameplate and punk stage details. The bottom remains flat for a
centered 25 mm NFC sticker.
