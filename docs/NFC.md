# NFC setup

Use non-metal, peel-and-stick 25 mm NFC tags. Program and test each tag before
applying it to a print.

The `release/v6/guides` folder includes:

- A finished 25 mm punk `TAP TO PLAY` label.
- A 20-up US Letter sheet for printing overlays on non-metal sticker stock.
- A small handoff card explaining that the collectible should be tapped.

An ordinary paper or non-metal vinyl overlay does not replace the NFC tag. It
is applied over the programmed tag so the interaction is visible. Never use
metallic foil or metal-backed label stock.

## Placement

- Cassette: center on the flat rear landing.
- Floppy: center inside the developed rear hub boundary. The v18 engraving
  deliberately remains outside the sticker contact circle.
- VHS: center on the flat rear shell.
- Trailer Swift: center on the flat underside of the circular base, inside the
  shallow placement ring.

Clean the plastic with isopropyl alcohol and allow it to dry completely before
applying the tag. Press firmly for 20 to 30 seconds, then test with both iPhone
and Android devices through the printed body.
