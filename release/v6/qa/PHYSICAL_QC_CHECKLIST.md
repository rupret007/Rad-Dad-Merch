# Retro Riot v6 physical quality-control checklist

Record filament, printer, nozzle, plate, date, and operator for every test batch.

## Identity and finish

- [ ] Cassette reads immediately as a compact cassette at arm's length.
- [ ] Floppy front and rear read immediately as a 3.5-inch disk.
- [ ] VHS reads immediately as VHS, not as a cassette.
- [ ] Trailer Swift stands squarely and the front nameplate is readable.
- [ ] RAD DAD and 3.69 MB are complete and legible.
- [ ] No strings, gaps, elephant foot, loose details, or sharp cleanup scars.

## Function

- [ ] Every media eyelet accepts the thickest production split ring.
- [ ] Every eyelet survives ten firm pull-and-twist cycles.
- [ ] Every programmed NFC tag scans before installation.
- [ ] Every installed tag scans three times on iPhone and three times on Android.
- [ ] TAP TO PLAY label remains fully adhered around its circumference.
- [ ] Trailer Swift base does not rock after the NFC tag is installed.

## Durability

- [ ] Each design survives a one-meter drop onto a hard floor three times.
- [ ] Media samples complete three days of pocket/key carry without eyelet damage.
- [ ] Trailer Swift hair, guitar, feet, and base remain intact after handling.

## Release decision

- [ ] PASS: approved for batch production.
- [ ] HOLD: defect documented and corrected before reprint.
